Scenesmith

Scenesmith is a personal creator studio. One person uses it, on their own computer, to make videos for their own channels and share them to their own accounts.

It writes original scripts, builds each video with AI-generated visuals, labels it as AI-generated where a platform asks for that, and shares it only to the accounts its owner connects and only when the owner turns sharing on.
