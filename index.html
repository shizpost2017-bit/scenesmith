Your own videos, scripted, built and published from one desktop studio.

Scenesmith is a desktop app for creators. It helps you plan a short video, write the script, build it with original visuals and voice-over, check its quality, and publish it to the TikTok and YouTube accounts you connect.

What it does

Scripts that help viewers

Every video starts from an original script with a clear hook, one useful takeaway and on-screen key points.

Original visuals

Builds each scene from AI-generated pictures and clips or licensed stock footage, then adds voice-over, captions and music.

Quality check

Checks loudness, silent gaps, black frames and whether the pictures match the script before anything is shared.

Publishing you control

Shares a finished video only to accounts you connected yourself, only when you turn sharing on for that account.

AI content labelled

Videos made with AI are marked as AI-generated content on platforms that support the label.

Runs on your computer

Your videos, scripts and account sign-ins stay on your own computer. There is no Scenesmith server holding your data.

How it works

  1. Pick a topic. Choose a subject for your channel, or let Scenesmith suggest one from your channel's niche.
  2. Review the script. Scenesmith writes the script and scene plan; you can edit any line.
  3. Build the video. It renders a vertical 9:16 video with voice-over, captions and visuals.
  4. Check it. An automatic quality check runs; you can watch the result in the app before sharing.
  5. Publish. Send it to your connected accounts.

Sharing to TikTok

Scenesmith uses TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API:

  1. You click Connect TikTok in Scenesmith and sign in on TikTok's own page. Scenesmith asks only for your basic profile (to show which account is connected) and permission to upload or post videos you create in Scenesmith.
  2. Before posting, Scenesmith shows the connected account, the caption, the privacy setting your TikTok account allows, and the AI-generated content label.
  3. The video is sent to TikTok either as a draft in your TikTok inbox (you finish and post it in the TikTok app) or posted to your profile with the privacy level you chose. Scenesmith then shows the post status reported by TikTok.
  4. You can disconnect at any time in TikTok under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions.

Scenesmith never reads your followers, messages or other people's content, and never posts anything you did not create and choose to share.

Who it is for

Individual creators who make short educational videos (car tips, money explainers, product guides, quizzes) and want one place to make and share them.
