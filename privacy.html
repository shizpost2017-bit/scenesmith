Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026.

Scenesmith is a personal tool used by one person on their own computer. It connects to the accounts its owner chooses, using each platform's own login page.

What it stores

Access tokens for the connected accounts, saved only on the owner's computer. They are used only to publish the owner's own videos and are never sent anywhere else.

What it does not do

It does not collect, sell or share personal data about other people. It does not read followers, messages or contacts, and it does not post anything the owner has not chosen to share.

Disconnecting

The owner can disconnect any platform at any time in that platform's own settings, or by deleting the saved token file on their computer.
